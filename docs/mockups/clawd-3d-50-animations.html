Clawd 3D — 50 animations

All 50 run on the same model: the locked silhouette stacked along Z (T5, 51% depth) with the locked warm-shadow flanks. Only the animation differs. Dead-on, every one of them resolves to the approved sprite.

Designed by 10 parallel lenses, then each batch audited adversarially — true peak rotation recomputed from the keyframes rather than trusted, illegal properties rejected, cross-lens duplicates dropped. 13 had wrongly-declared peaks; 20 had inconsistent transform function lists across their own keyframes and were normalised, without which the browser falls back to matrix decomposition and animates a different path than the one authored.

Dropped in curation

  • id-nodding-off — Duplicate of ch-nod-off (same doze-and-catch gesture, same motivation 9). Kept the CHARACTER version: deeper pitch (36 vs 25), a 7.5s loop instead of 12s, and a 0.45s snap-back that sells mass better.
  • ch-double-take — Duplicate of al-double-take (identical look-away-then-whip-back beat). Kept the ALERT version: higher depth (9 vs 8), HUD-safe, and tied to a concrete trigger (a refresh landing a worse number) rather than a vague return-to-machine.
  • ch-puffed-up — Duplicate of ce-take-a-bow (both are 'present each side on a milestone' showcases). Take-a-bow wins on every axis: motivation 9 vs 4, depth 9 vs 7, and half the runtime.
  • st-coin-settle — Duplicate of ce-victory-spin in the spin-and-settle family, and the weaker read: motivation 5 vs 7, 720 degrees of yaw with no character motive behind it. One airborne revolution per set is enough.
  • ce-backflip — Third full-rotation flip alongside tr-tumble-land (168 pitch, motivation 8) and st-flip-stick (360 pitch, motivation 8), and the lowest-motivated of the three at 6. Its axis coverage is already provided by both survivors.
  • st-victory-roll — Weakest celebration in the set (motivation 6, depth 5) and redundant with ce-shimmy/ce-fist-pump as the small-win beat. An aileron roll is a camera move, not a character choice.
  • st-sentry-sweep — Duplicate of al-nervous-scan (both are held-pose scanning loops for an unacknowledged alert). Nervous-scan is the better version: motivation 8 vs 6, and its anxiety motive beats a 360-degree compass sweep that reads as a turntable with stops.
  • ph-gyro-precess — Lowest motivation in the entire pool (2) and the closest thing to a bare turntable; the working pool already has nine better-motivated loops across three tempos.
  • st-grand-reveal — Showroom piece: motivation 3, 4.2s of self-presentation for a first-launch beat that tr-unfold and tr-deal-in already cover more cheaply. Rule 3 explicitly disfavours this trade.
  • tr-swap-flip — Lowest-motivated transition (3) and a direct duplicate of tr-tumble-land / st-corkscrew-dive as the cover-the-skin-swap move; a card turning over adds no read the tumble does not.
