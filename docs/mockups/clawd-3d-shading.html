Clawd 3D — flank shading fix

Geometry is untouched. Only the per-slice colour changes. Front slice stays exactly #e17956 in every variant, so dead-on it is still the locked sprite.

Two problems with the old ramp. One: it is linear to 0.26 brightness, but the flank you see when the model turns is the stacked edge of many slices at once — a linear ramp makes that flank a gradient dissolving into black, when a real solid shows a flat side face at one steady tone. Two: it multiplies toward black. Shadow on an orange body shifts warm, toward brown-red. Multiplying to black drains the hue out of the whole side.

Each card shows a held 3/4 angle (judge the flank) and the live turn, plus the raw ramp as a strip — front slice at the left edge, back slice at the right.
