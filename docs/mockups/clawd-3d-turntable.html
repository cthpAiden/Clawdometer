D1 turntable — thickness iteration

Thickness is slices × step. The visible seam between slices is step × sin(rotation), so raising step to get depth just produces banding at the extremes. Every card below therefore holds step near 1.3px and adds slices. Depth ratio is quoted against the 161px sprite width.

Depth ladder

T1 is the current D1 for reference. Each step up roughly doubles the depth ratio.
